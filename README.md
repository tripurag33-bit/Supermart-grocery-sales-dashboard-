📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes 9,994 grocery sales records from 2015 to 2018 using Microsoft Excel. The raw data is transformed into an interactive dashboard to monitor sales, profit, order volume, discounts, product categories, and regional performance.

The dashboard includes KPI cards, category and regional comparisons, and yearly and monthly sales trends to provide meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Sales: 14,956,982
- Total Profit: 3,747,121.20
- Total Orders: 9,994
- Average Discount: 22.68%
- Profit Margin: 25.05%

🔍 Key Business Insights

- Total sales reached 14,956,982, with a total profit of 3,747,121.20.
- Eggs, Meat & Fish was the highest-selling category, generating 2,267,401 in sales.
- The West region was the leading region, contributing 4,798,743 in sales.
- Health Drinks was the highest-performing sub-category, with sales of 1,051,439.
- Annual sales increased throughout the reported period, with 2018 recording the highest sales of 4,977,512 and profit of 1,244,182.88.
- The correlation between discount and profit was 0.000, indicating no meaningful linear relationship in this dataset.
